How you raise a frequency

You don't measure it. You come present, and you lift from there.

A mantra is an instrument for the mind, and its sounds are words or music. It can take you up, and it can take you down. So you do not let the one in your head be a bad one. You make it empowering, and then you walk it.

  • Come present. The laptop. Your hands. The keys. The ring on your finger.
  • Feel your body. The whole of it. It is yours, and you've got it.
  • Be grateful you've got it, and that other people get to fall in love with it.
  • Your eyes go softer. You feel a little better.
  • And now, only now, you go up. Way up.

Words are frequencies

Every word holds someone, and reaches somewhere.

Left to right is who the word holds: only me, me against another, us together, or all of it as one. Bottom to top is how high it reaches.

The mantra is the walk across it: from low and alone, in mine, to high and open, in one.
