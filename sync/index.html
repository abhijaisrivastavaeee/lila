Both of you: hold your phone close to your own speaker, then tap below on both phones. It plays two soft chirps and uses the mics to line the speakers up by itself. (This tunes songs played through this app, not Spotify.)

Line up this speaker

0 ms

Bluetooth speakers each add their own delay, and the II and III are different. Slide until this phone's sound meets the other one. It is saved on this phone.

About Spotify: two phones can't be held in perfect sync while each streams Spotify on its own, so in the same room you'd hear a slight echo. For tight sync the app plays one shared track on both phones. Use the click to calibrate, then play a song file through here. Spotify to a single speaker still works the normal way.
